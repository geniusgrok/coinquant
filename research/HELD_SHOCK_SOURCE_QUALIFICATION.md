# Original held-shock wallet input qualification

This checks whether the frozen historical **simulation** can be paired in the already selected 2020Q1 and 2021Q2 core periods. It does not change the held-shock rule, the manual starts, or any wallet, and it does not measure an executable exit.

## Original source contract

The archived producer is `backtest-source/coin-tooling/research/session_market.py` and `session_exchange.py` at simulation commit `c6223a5d1bd6ae46fd192668335cee31630ff33e`. `TradePrints` requires official Binance USD-M BTCUSDT **daily `aggTrades` ZIPs with `.CHECKSUM`**. It parses aggregation ID, price, quantity and millisecond transaction time; a missing day is unknown. The venue also reads completed four-hour perpetual trade candles, one-minute trade and mark candles, funding settlements, the archived FX/schedule/features and warmup files. Its loader requires the complete 2020–August 2026 four-hour series and all monthly funding files through September 2026 **before** either cold quarter begins.

The [Binance public-data specification](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/blob/master/README.md) identifies USD-M `aggTrades` as aggregate ID, price, quantity, first/last trade ID, time and buyer-maker flag; USD-M `trades` supplies trade ID, price, quantity, quote quantity, time and buyer-maker flag. Official fixed quarter-start `trades` ZIP URLs also returned HTTP 200, but raw trades are not the frozen parser's aggregate-ID input. Neither file supplies contemporaneous bid/ask levels, displayed depth, queue position or our order acknowledgement/fill. The old venue derives a one-level “book” from the last print and the previous minute's volume, and its hosted stop closes at the trigger with zero modeled slippage. Exact public prints can reproduce **that proxy**, not prove that its assumed liquidity or stop price was executable.

## Source recovery performed

The previous public-print vault retained archive **receipts**, not ZIP bodies. Its 33 receipts in 2020Q1 and 23 in 2021Q2 match the 56 names and SHA-256 digests in the published 795-session simulated account's `loaded_print_files`. To avoid transmitting the private manual-session selection, the retrieval requested **every public calendar day** in the two fixed quarters: 91 daily ZIPs for each quarter. All 182 ZIPs passed their official `.CHECKSUM`; all 56 historically loaded ZIPs additionally match both archived digests. Compressed download totals were 480,577,252 bytes in 2020Q1 and 3,354,502,960 bytes in 2021Q2. No ZIP or order/account detail was committed. Two fixed public quarter-start files were also streamed to validate one CSV member, seven fields, positive price/quantity, millisecond timestamps within the UTC day and nondecreasing `(time, aggregate ID)` order; they contained 71,359 and 1,227,810 rows. The old parser's expected columns and exact file identity therefore qualify for **proxy replay** in these quarters.

The original 80 four-hour monthly ZIP bytes were locally present and SHA matched to the archived metadata. Subsequent explicit authorization allowed retrieval of the remaining **81 monthly funding** ZIPs, **10 quarter-specific one-minute trade/mark** ZIPs and **19 daily September 2026 four-hour** ZIPs. Two required mark months were already present. A different archived January 2020 trade-minute ZIP did not match this producer's original SHA and was not reused. All 110 new ZIPs matched the original archived hashes and sizes; archived official sidecar text matched all 192 required source names and digests. The unchanged loader completed with no four-hour or funding grid gap. The paired result is in `HELD_SHOCK_PAIR_RESULT.md`.

## Decision boundary

The first broad-fetch attempt was rejected by automatic approval review as beyond the then-authorized quarter scope and stopped before execution. The user then explicitly approved the precise 110-ZIP public range; the narrower source restoration above used that approval. The old loader's fixed all-year funding check explains why those out-of-quarter files were needed, while no loader code was changed. Market-input qualification now permits the recorded **historical proxy** pair, but native stop slippage, executable depth and live fills still require separate native evidence. No adoption or `main` change follows.
