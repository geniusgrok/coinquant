2026-10-07. Independent, read-only source check on the Coinquant research branch based on main `f3fe9b8be32fbe8cce324b35c57a06fc929a4958`. This does **not** replace the frozen CoinGecko USDT source, combine the two series, test any trade outcome, or change production code.

## Source identity and private receipt

- Source ID: `coinmetrics_community_usdt_capest_price_v1`. The sole fixed public request is [`community-api.coinmetrics.io` asset metrics](https://community-api.coinmetrics.io/v4/timeseries/asset-metrics?assets=usdt&metrics=CapMrktEstUSD,PriceUSD&frequency=1d&start_time=2020-01-01&end_time=2026-09-30&page_size=10000) for `usdt`, `CapMrktEstUSD,PriceUSD`, `1d`, 2020-01-01 through 2026-09-30, page size 10,000. No API key or account endpoint was used.
- An independent HTTP 200 response was received at 2026-10-07 15:27:16 UTC. Its original 336,432 bytes and a separate metadata receipt are kept privately with `0600` permissions. SHA-256 `18d511062eb636677960843006325a7f448f176faca0fbcddb8a9459ae4f99da` matches the prior independent fetch. The response has 2,465 unique, consecutive 00:00 UTC daily labels, two non-null positive decimal-string metrics per `usdt` row, no extra page, and no missing dates. The latest label is 2026-09-30. No raw observations are committed or reproduced here.
- [Coin Metrics' definition](https://docs.coinmetrics.io/network-data/network-data-overview/market/market-capitalization#d) makes `CapMrktEstUSD` an **estimated circulating supply × PriceUSD**, both in USD terms; `PriceUSD` is the daily close at 00:00 UTC. `Decimal(CapMrktEstUSD) / Decimal(PriceUSD)` yields estimated circulating **USDT units**. `CapMrktCurUSD` uses total current supply including illiquid inventory and is a different field. Only the `usdt` asset row is used; no `usdt_eth`, `usdt_trx` or other chain amount is added. Coin Metrics says its circulating supply comes from third-party APIs including CoinGecko, so this is **not independent confirmation of supply**.

The source response contains event labels and values, not their original release/receipt times or historical versions. The only proven `observed_at` is the 2026-10-07 fetch. `historical_first_receipt = null` and `historical_pit = false` remain explicit. A later response could revise historical values; the saved hash identifies this one snapshot, not an immutable vintage or a 2020-era publication. The 00:00 label and a present-day full archive do not prove any row was usable at a past Coinquant manual start.

## Current CoinGecko comparison, for source diagnosis only

The existing private CoinGecko 365-day response contains 365 completed midnight daily points after excluding its current intraday point. **358** dates overlap this Coin Metrics snapshot, from 2025-10-08 through 2026-09-30. Their labels both read 00:00 UTC. Same-label median absolute relative gaps are **0.01928%** for market cap, **0.01853%** for USDT/USD price and effectively zero for price-divided estimated units; the respective 95th percentile gaps are **0.16062%**, **0.06523%** and **0.16743%**. Estimated units are within `1e-10` relative on 213/358 dates. These close levels are consistent with partly shared upstream supply; they do not prove identical release clocks or independent validation.

As a *label-alignment diagnostic*, median absolute cap gaps are 0.03220%, **0.01928%**, and 0.02273% when comparing Coin Metrics date `t` with CoinGecko `t−1`, `t`, and `t+1`, respectively. Same-label levels align best of these three fixed checks, but the datasets have different construction. Even the simple two-day **cap rise direction differs on 81/356** comparable days; the inferred-unit rise direction differs on 43/356, and Coin Metrics cap and its own inferred-unit direction differ on 73/356. A cap sign is therefore not interchangeable with a supply-flow sign. None of these counts uses BTC prices, strategy P/L, account fills, or selected trading thresholds.

The Coin Metrics calendar covers all 795 original manual starts, and 116 fall within the 358-day overlap calendar. **Zero** starts have a verified historical first receipt for this source. Calendar coverage is not point-in-time eligibility. No source was merged into the existing CoinGecko sample or used to backfill its missing history.

## License and decision

[Coin Metrics' Community API documentation](https://github.com/coinmetrics/product-docs/blob/master/docs/access-our-data/api/README.md#free-tier-community-api) says the keyless data is free for **non-commercial** use under [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). That license requires attribution and does not permit commercial use. This report credits Coin Metrics, links the license and source, and adapts only private data into aggregate diagnostics. The cited documentation does **not** explicitly grant use in a money-seeking automated trading system or settle whether the user's intended trading use is non-commercial. Personal use alone is not an exemption established by the text. Its additional Terms of Use link currently redirects to a Talos product page, leaving no clearer grant here.

**Decision:** Source schema, units, continuity and numeric comparability are established for this captured snapshot; historical point-in-time availability and trading-use rights are **not** established. Keep this source separate and research-only. Do not compute candidate returns, wire it into Coinquant, publish the raw series, acquire a key, register, pay or accept new terms. Further economic work requires a clear right for the intended use and original release/version evidence at the actual irregular manual starts (or genuinely new future receipts), followed by a separately frozen source-specific hypothesis. The incumbent strategy and original 150% CAGR / below-50% drawdown reference remain unchanged and unproven by this source check.
