This follows `ENTRY_SHOCK_DEFER_PAIR_FREEZE.md` without changing its signal, threshold, order behavior or observation clock. The existing paired wallets establish only the 2020Q1 and 2021Q2 selected windows. This check closes the simpler claim that the other two shocked macro fills would automatically disappear. It is a read-only original-path and conditional-price calculation, **not** a new wallet result.

`entry_shock_later_path.py` joins the already attributed 50 original flat-to-flat fills to all 795 archived terminal reports and the archived offline financial ledger. It checks the producer's complete/audited/non-native identity, the original SHA-qualified Binance USD-M four-hour ZIPs, and the SHA-qualified one-minute **perpetual mark** ZIPs. It evaluates the unchanged completed-day downside-RMS test only at actual manual starts, confirms the same owned protected campaign, the original point-in-time DFII10 eligibility, and the original 4h primary model's active opportunity. The original model file is byte-identical to the archived producer's model. The script prints quarter aggregates, never account, order or exact session identifiers. Neither script nor report fetches data or opens a venue.

All five surviving later starts in the latter two rows had the original macro campaign owned and fully protected, the original DFII10 input eligible, and no active unconsumed primary long. This establishes **action space**, not a counterfactual fill: a deferred account might retain the macro opportunity and enter when the statistic clears. The 2020Q3 and 2023Q1 original exits came about 140 and 59 hours after their first possible reentry starts, respectively. The 2020Q3 original exit was ordinary; the 2023Q1 exit was the confirmed final native-simulated stop. A later manual start by itself is insufficient; these additional opportunity and input checks are the reason those two starts remain plausible.

For a narrowly stated price bound, hold each original later inventory quantity, original later exit print, remaining signed funding, and 0.075% taker commission on both BUY and SELL fixed. Solve the BUY price that would make that *single* delayed trade break even. The last fully completed 1-minute perpetual mark before the possible reentry start is above that break-even BUY by **0.694% in 2020Q3** and **2.906% in 2023Q1**. Buying at those marks and following the original exits still loses after fees and funding, about **0.69%** and **2.83%** of delayed buy notional, respectively. The 2023Q1 break-even BUY is even below the original final stop trigger; an unchanged above-stop entry followed by that same original stop print cannot break even. An actual spread, queue wait, buy slippage, stop gap or worse exit could deepen the loss. A different protected fill, earlier stop, changed wallet sizing or later winner could also change the outcome. The mark is not an executable offer, and the original producer's stop prints have zero modeled trigger slippage. These percentages are arithmetic requirements for a hypothetical unchanged exit, **not** calibrated safe-slippage limits.

The archived 2023Q1 cold baseline has the same early macro first fill and stop time/price as the 795-session original, but a differently sized independent wallet. Its accepted held-exit candidate sold the early macro sooner; a later winning primary BUY was then about **3.15% larger**, accounting for part of the previously reported final-wallet gain. That candidate is a different action from entry deferral, and its recorded baseline and candidate source-head labels differ. It cannot supply the absent defer wallet or prove primary capital release. The two new complete paired windows together improve their own terminal wallet by about 124 simulated USDT; roughly 63% comes from one window. The four selected original shocked losses are also concentrated: about 61% of their subtotal comes from 2023Q1. Neither concentration is a future hit-rate or an independent annual result. In the two *unpaired* windows, no later primary opportunity was shown before possible reentry, but primary sizing after a new macro exit, overall wealth, drawdown and worst loss remain unknown.

The proper no-macro comparator is zero macro participation, while the frozen *deferral* can resume participation. In the two already paired windows their actions coincided; in the two later windows they need not. The archived full no-macro path failed its drawdown/slippage requirements, so its selected trade avoidance does not qualify the present rule. Conditional mark-price savings versus the original entry are less than the loss removed by a no-macro veto in the two later windows, and cannot be booked as paired savings. The frozen gate remains unsupported for `main`; no threshold change, permanent consume variant, 795 replay or claim toward 150% CAGR / <50% drawdown follows.

The accepted 2023Q1 cold baseline and held-exit terminal wallets were reused, but there is no retained per-cycle baseline SQLite for that quarter and no retained source-qualified original one-minute **trade** or daily aggregate-trade ZIP bodies for either unpaired fixed quarter in this workspace. With no executable quote or stop-gap observations and no complete paired later path, adoption remains blocked on evidence.
