# CFTC positioning pilot, frozen before reading campaign classifications

Source boundary: CFTC Public Reporting TFF Futures Only, CME BITCOIN code 133741. The platform's row creation timestamp is checked against the official 2023 ION and 2025 shutdown release announcements. Rows bulk imported before September 2022 cannot establish their first publication; a row with a later modification cannot establish its original values. Use only rows whose current creation and modification timestamps are identical and whose creation precedes the original manual entry session. Never substitute report Tuesday or an assumed Friday. This conditions a historical development study; it does not prove a receipt-time live feed.

Independent hypothesis: a week-to-week fall in `(leveraged-money longs - leveraged-money shorts) / open interest` may mark additional futures selling pressure during a primary long entry. This is CME participant positioning, not Binance order flow, and some short futures may hedge a cash-and-carry trade. Therefore the sign alone is a weak prior, subject to immediate falsification. The sole proposed action is to **skip a new flat primary BUY** when the latest two published, unchanged CFTC rows have a negative change in that ratio. Do not modify macro entries, held positions, protection, seven-day expiry, leverage, thresholds or manual start times. No threshold tuning or alternate lag is permitted after seeing outcomes.

Cheapest falsification: join the original 50 flat-to-flat simulated intervals by their actual first manual entry start to the two latest qualified reports, and inspect only post-platform-launch primary entries. Show missing pairs and source age without inventing a freshness cutoff. Compare sign-negative (hypothetically skipped) with sign-nonnegative entries by number, net P/L including fees and funding, winning and losing intervals, signal age and calendar segment. Report the winners a skip would sacrifice. A favorable retrospective split is **not** an account improvement: subsequent wallet sizing, still-active opportunities at later manual starts, native stops and path drawdown need a paired simulator and qualification before implementation. If the small check loses important winners, fails to exclude material losers, concentrates in one regime, or has too few independent entries, reject this mechanism without a 795-session replay.

Original target remains 150% CAGR and <50% drawdown. No strategy or account operation follows this freeze.

## Result: rejected at the cheap check

The fixed public query returned 212 CME BTC rows from September 13, 2022 onward; 211 had unchanged platform row timestamps. The original simulation contained 17 post-launch primary entry intervals; all 17 had two qualified reports already created before the original manual entry. The newest report was 4–16 calendar days old. No report was dated by an assumed Tuesday-to-Friday lag and no manual-session timestamp was sent in an API request.

The predeclared negative-change rule would skip **6** intervals: **4 winners** with about 59,985 USDT combined original net profit and **2 losers** with about 33,927 USDT combined original net loss. Their net contribution was **+26,058 USDT** after original fees and funding, so dropping the six is adverse even under the overly generous isolated-interval arithmetic. The other 11 intervals contained 8 winners and 3 losers. The skipped set had positive net contribution in 2022–24 and negative net contribution in 2025–26; the sign split is not stable across the two calendar segments. All figures are from the original *simulated* intervals, not a new wallet path or native trades.

Decision: **reject this directional CFTC gate**. It discards substantial winners, does not have stable independent evidence, and a full 795-session wallet replay cannot rescue its failed cheap premise. No threshold, lag or alternate sign was optimized. The official platform clock has value for future conditional research, but its existence is not alpha and cannot establish the original 150% CAGR / <50% drawdown goal.
