# Binance BTCUSDT OI: conditional historical screen failed

**Decision:** Close the one frozen hypothesis that completed 24-hour BTC-denominated open-interest contraction adds protective-stop information for a new Coinquant long after price, volatility, volume and funding controls. The observed relationship is in the opposite direction. No entry filter, wallet replay, 795-session run, or `main` change follows. The original 150% CAGR / under-50% drawdown target is still unproven.

## Qualification and frozen test

The archived [replacement contract](https://github.com/geniusgrok/coinquant/blob/bf1210e3fd718af1a5801143349e7cf7725be2b6/research/replacement-spec.json) permits historical *development* information screening but says it is not prospective alpha or native qualification. This check retains the [strict-version source result](BINANCE_OI_SOURCE_PILOT.md): only **four** original independent opportunities have both needed current ZIP and CHECKSUM object versions dated before their actual manual starts, with one original protective stop. Later retrieval cannot turn old revised files into point-in-time receipts.

Before source/outcome association, the [single directional mechanism and controls](BINANCE_OI_CONDITIONAL_FREEZE.md) were fixed: larger `−log(OI_prior_UTC_day_close / OI_day_before_close)` may indicate weaker participation behind a fresh long. `sum_open_interest` is the BTC quantity, not the price-driven USDT value. It is neither forced-liquidation data nor a directional order-flow reading. The old unqualified OI + liquidation + spot-absorption threshold route was not rerun. One original filled flat-to-flat campaign at its first actual BUY is one observation. Original durable terminal stop identity determines the protective-stop label. No later manual session was promoted into a new observation.

The predeclared controls use completed original SHA-qualified Binance USD-M 4h data and funding records: prior-day BTC log return, 20-day realized RMS, prior-day log futures quote volume, latest pre-start funding rate, primary/macro model, and 2020–2023 versus 2024–2026 period. Both OI score and stop label are residualized on intercept and these controls. One-sided positive partial Pearson correlation is assessed by 10,000 within-period label permutations, fixed seed 0. The frozen minimum was 30 independent campaigns, 10 stops, 10 non-stops and at least three stops per period. A pass additionally needed p≤0.05 and a positive residual score difference between stops and other exits in both periods. This screen has no entry threshold or wallet payoff.

## Public source and parser evidence

The requests covered the **fixed public calendar** 2020-09-01 through 2026-09-30, independent of the private manual-start schedule. All **2,221** official [Binance BTCUSDT USD-M daily metrics](https://data.binance.vision/?prefix=data/futures/um/daily/metrics/BTCUSDT/) ZIP/CHECKSUM pairs were fetched once and passed the published SHA-256 sidecars. The retained raw ZIPs, sidecars, response headers, UTC receipt times, frozen specification and read-only local screening script remain outside Git. The original 99 four-hour and 81 funding ZIPs used here matched the existing original-source digests; no futures history or account session was replayed.

Each metrics day was sorted, identical repeated rows were deduplicated, and the original symbol, time grid and positive finite OI quantity were checked. Among the 2,221 days, **2,127** have all 288 unique five-minute slots and valid OI, **71** lack a slot, and **23** have zero/invalid OI. Source quirks include **68,256** extra duplicate rows and **137** days whose file rows are out of order. Blank *unused* long/short ratio fields appeared on many days, especially in 2022. A first, overly broad all-numeric-fields parser would have left only 32 campaigns / 8 stops. Before calculating any score/outcome statistic, the parser rule was explicitly amended to validate the **used** OI field while retaining complete-grid and checksum requirements. The first support result and amendment are retained locally; no rule was tuned to the observed score relation.

Of the 50 original independent filled campaigns, ten lack the two predecessor archive days and one more fails the completed OI-day check. The final conditional historical sample is **39 campaigns and 10 original protective stops**: 24/4 in 2020–2023 and 15/6 in 2024–2026. It meets the frozen information-screen minimum. It is still repeatedly used development history, not an independent forward sample.

## Single result and boundary

Partial correlation is **−0.1980**; positive one-sided permutation **p=0.8962**. Residual OI-contraction score differences, stop minus other exits, are **−0.03784** in the first period and **−0.00443** in the second. All three signs oppose the frozen positive-stop-risk mechanism. No opposite-sign rule, alternative lag, threshold search, wallet pair or cost backtest was tried. Since the information gate failed, prospective economic benefit cannot be claimed and the current strategy remains unchanged.

The [Binance public-data guide](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data) explicitly permits archive updates and has no old per-row first-publication/version chain. The current ZIP sidecars authenticate the files **now**; even the 39 conditional sample cannot be treated as 39 historically available current versions. The original execution producer's IOC depth, latency and zero-slip stop proxies remain uncalibrated by native observations. Any future OI research would require independent, timestamped receipts and a distinct predeclared hypothesis, rather than reusing this failed expression.
