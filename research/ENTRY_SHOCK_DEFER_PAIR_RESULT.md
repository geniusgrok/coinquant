# Entry-side shock consistency: fixed two-quarter result

This follows the separately committed diagnostic and candidate freezes. The old default has **no** downside-shock admission condition; the archived held-exit overlay intentionally waits for a confirmed, protected `hold`. The three accepted same-session reversals are therefore consistent with the overlay's literal rule, not an original model or order-identity bug. The new expression asks the different economic question of avoiding an entry while the *same already-completed* shock is visible. It defers rather than consumes the macro opportunity, so a later actual manual start may still enter it.

## Complete opportunity check before pairing

`entry_shock_consistency.py` reads the 50 already attributed original first fills, exact SHA-qualified original USD-M four-hour monthly ZIPs and the two fresh baseline SQLite observation tables without an adapter or wallet. At each actual entry session it applies the same `held_risk.measure` formula to the last 20 completed futures days. No entry session crossed a UTC day boundary. Of 20 macro first fills, **four** met the shock condition and all four were eventual net losers, totaling **−2,327.15 simulated USDT** in the old 795-session wallet. The other 16 macro fills included **nine winners and seven nonwinners**; their net subtotal was −761.74 USDT. Of 30 primary first fills, 28 had complete futures history and none triggered; two early primary fills lack the required original prior 20 days (one winner, one nonwinner). This is outcome-conditioned development history, not evidence that a live filter identifies future losers.

The two fixed paired quarters' **every baseline observation cycle** had four distinct flat entry opportunities: two source-gap primary opportunities in 2020Q1 and one shocked macro opportunity in each quarter. All four eventually filled in the baseline. The 2020 macro made one zero-fill IOC and retried the same opportunity within its original session; this is not a separate opportunity. Both shocked entries later reached protected `hold` within that same session. The 2023Q1 archived candidate has one confirmed cap/reduction but no retained per-cycle SQLite here, so it is not promoted to this full decision-coverage count.

## One registered action and paired wallet outcomes

Only `entry-shock-defer` was tested: at an otherwise qualified flat macro `enter`, a ready 5/20 downside RMS ratio above 1.5 suppresses that BUY, preserves the opportunity, and reevaluates only at actual manual cycles/starts. The exact archived market/account producer, source-qualified inputs, 300-second starts, original fees/funding/FX, ownership and native-shape protection were retained. The research hook refuses non-offline venues. Two new serial candidate wallets were compared with the already finished fresh baselines and held-exit wallets. All six have identical per-quarter start lists and matching daily/features/FX/price input digests, passed the original financial audit, are complete with zero pending intents, and have `native_verified=false`. The two new candidate journals each contain one deferred macro opportunity; 39 read-only polling cycles in its original session produced no BUY, and no later same-opportunity entry occurred in these two quarters. Raw journal identities, orders and session clocks remain local.

## Why this does not yet qualify for `main`

Two other shocked macro first fills in the complete original history occur outside the qualified paired quarters. For both, at later **actual** manual starts within seven days, the completed-day shock had cleared while the original reports still showed the owned macro campaign, its DFII10 row remained eligible, and a read-only reconstruction of the qualified four-hour primary model showed no active competing primary. A *flat* deferred wallet could therefore present a macro entry again; the old realized losses cannot be booked as four automatically avoided trades. Original reports cannot prove the counterfactual's fresh capital, quote, order fill or subsequent wallet path. Testing those additional wallets would need further original minute/print sources and would still reuse selected development history. No private start was used to request data, and no extra source was fetched here.

The fixed pair passes its narrow **mechanical** question: entry-side deferral prevents the two observed same-session round trips with less research runtime machinery than a durable held reduction and favorable local proxy wealth/tail tradeoffs. It does **not** establish independent alpha, superiority to the failed no-macro path over the full manual schedule, native execution, or the original 150% CAGR / <50% drawdown target. Do not port the hook to production, add a permanent consume variant, tune 1.5, reschedule starts or run the 795 wallet to rescue this selected result. The next decisive evidence would be qualified executable costs and a source-faithful comparison at the *later eligible starts* (or genuinely forward manual sessions), including winners lost and the primary path. No such evidence is claimed by the present pair.
