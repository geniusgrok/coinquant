## Same-session meaning and archived third treatment

The two new journals and the already archived 2023Q1 accepted cold journal each contain exactly **one cap and one confirmed reduction**: three treated macro campaigns, not three independent later-life shock discoveries. Matching each journal to its own wallet's first BUY and actual 300-second manual session gives cap times **53.0 / 71.8 / 96.6 seconds** after session start and confirmed reductions **68.6 / 87.4 / 112.2 seconds** after start, in 2020Q1 / 2021Q2 / 2023Q1 order. The last BUY preceded the cap by about **25 / 63 / 63 seconds**. All three completed-daily input windows ended before that BUY. The archived rule deliberately permits this: once an entry is owned and protected, the next cycle calls it `hold`; it has no minimum age. Thus the observed treatment is a near-immediate reversal of an entry made with an already-known downside statistic. A pre-entry veto might be simpler, but these selected losers do not establish its effect on retained or missed winners; the earlier blanket no-macro path failed. No veto is inferred or implemented.

The reused 2023Q1 accepted candidate improved its simulated quarter net CNY return **211.2163% → 220.9808%** (+9.7645 pp), with unchanged **25.0191%** path MDD and a negligible adverse change in sampled daily USDT ES5. It has ten trades in each wallet. Its six BUY timestamps and prices match the baseline, but a later BUY quantity and later SELL quantity change because the early exit leaves more capital. Its **142.18 USDT** final-wallet gain therefore includes later sizing effects; it is not the cash saved by the first reduction alone. That old comparison used different recorded source heads for baseline and candidate, although the held-rule file is byte-identical to the newer producer and the fresh 2020/2021 baselines reproduce their older baseline receipts. Treat 2023 as reused development evidence, not a fresh same-head pair or out-of-sample confirmation.

For a narrower fee/cost check, compare only each changed early SELL with the original later SELL for that same quantity, attribute funding paid between those exits, and charge the original taker fee on both. Conditional on the old simulator and unchanged remaining path, the direct advantages are about **69.35 / 42.23 / 86.59 USDT**. The extra adverse *early SELL price* needed to erase those direct amounts is about **2.66% / 4.61% / 6.39%** of that early exit notional. These are arithmetic cushions, **not executable slippage limits**: the producer uses last-print liquidity, zero market slippage and zero stop-trigger slippage, and has no contemporaneous bid, depth, queue or latency observation. The 2020 pair also retains its 29 bounded hindsight-gap minutes. Do not replace the 2023 direct advantage with its larger final-wallet gain when judging cost tolerance.

Current `session.run` can revisit a protected entry within one bounded 300-second manual run, as the offline pairs demonstrate, but `main` has no held-shock policy. The archived implementation explicitly refuses non-offline adapters. An arbitrary manual start can leave insufficient time for entry, readback and a later held cycle; after the process exits, only native stop/take protection acts. Porting the candidate would require ownership-safe durable reductions and separate native validation. There is no unattended strategy clock, and these pairs provide no native fill or stop-gap evidence. With only three selected same-session treatments and no qualified executable quote/depth or authorized native observations, another chosen historical wallet or a full 795 replay would not resolve the adoption decision.

**Decision:** the two newly supported regimes show local net and 2021 tail improvement under the frozen simulator, so the earlier “no additional treatment” explanation was wrong. Adoption is still unsupported. All three accepted treatments reverse entries within their original manual sessions using information already available at entry; neither a held-exit advantage under native execution nor a safe pre-entry filter has been shown. Do not multiply these selected quarter gains into the 795-session CAGR, label the original 150% CAGR / <50% drawdown target met, or change `main`, Demo, live accounts, leverage, starts or expiry. Independent evidence of executable exit costs and native protection, or qualified forward manual sessions that also count missed winners, is needed before reconsidering implementation.
