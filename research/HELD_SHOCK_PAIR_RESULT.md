It thus missed no *observed* winning entry in these two cold wallets, but this does not bound missed winners at other manual starts. Total trade count stayed 26 in 2020Q1 and 2 in 2021Q2. The 2021 result is mostly avoidance of an approximately five-hour losing hold by reversing shortly after entry; it is not evidence that an offline seven-day decision suddenly became executable.

Adoption is still unsupported. The candidate's durable held-cap runtime is also materially more complex than the current simple default, and these same-session reversals could be an entry-filter question only after a separate causal freeze and missed-winner check; no such new rule is made here. Do not multiply these selected quarter gains into the 795-session CAGR, label the original 150% CAGR / <50% drawdown target met, or change `main`, Demo, live accounts, leverage, starts or expiry.
