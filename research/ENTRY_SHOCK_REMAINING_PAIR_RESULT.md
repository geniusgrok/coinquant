# Entry-shock deferral: remaining two frozen quarter pairs

`ENTRY_SHOCK_REMAINING_PAIR_FREEZE.md` fixed the rule and both windows before these candidate results. The original detached producer remained clean at `8617bef6dc236389a6dc254348adef5ad03c763b`; the research gate is byte-identical to the first two pairs. Only the original manual starts were used: 32 in 2020Q3 and 26 in 2023Q1, with 300-second sessions and independent 10,000 CNY cold cash per wallet. Each quarter received one fresh baseline and one `entry-shock-defer` wallet, run serially. The accepted older 2023Q1 baseline was **exactly reproduced** in final wealth, MDD, fees, funding and every trade by the fresh baseline, despite its older source-head label.

The fixed public corpus contains 182 daily `aggTrades` ZIPs and twelve one-minute trade/mark monthly ZIPs, including two reused original mark bodies. All 194 bodies matched official `.CHECKSUM` SHA-256; the 69 bodies present in the original producer's source manifest also matched its archived SHA-256 and size. The manifest and raw ZIPs remain local. Within each paired quarter, baseline and candidate have identical source/specification, price/features/FX/schedule digests, loaded minute and print digests, and actual manual-start sequence. All four wallets are complete with financial audit passed, no pending intents and no unresolved session execution. They retain `native_verified=false` and `qualification=NOT_QUALIFIED`: this is the original simulated print/stop proxy, not an account or native execution acceptance.

Both opportunities were deferred in **two** actual manual sessions each. The shock later cleared, the same unconsumed macro campaign reappeared, and a BUY filled after about **38 hours** in 2020Q3 or **120 hours** in 2023Q1. Thus the original losses were not simply avoided. The candidate's reentry session had 20/23 owned, fully protected observation readbacks, respectively; the final simulated stop trigger matched its baseline, and no account action was unresolved. In 2020Q3 the first nine baseline trades are unchanged, while the last macro BUY fills later and slightly larger before the same ordinary SELL. Its after-cost macro loss shrinks, explaining the +5.55 wallet difference. The candidate saves about 38 held hours and 0.37 USDT of modeled funding, but adds 0.05 USDT fees and 65 USDT turnover. Its sampled ES5 gets slightly worse, while MDD is unchanged.

In 2023Q1 the first six trades are unchanged. The delayed macro entry fills in two fragments with **2.42×** the baseline quantity, then reaches the *same* final stop print. The original macro loss-to-stop sizing cap kept modeled price loss approximately constant: a lower entry closer to that unchanged stop allowed more units. The candidate's macro net result worsens by about **1.88 simulated USDT**, mostly from higher commission. The later winning primary entry is still made at the original time and price, but its quantity is about **0.2% lower**; that winner's net contribution falls about **3.47 USDT**. These two differences reconcile the wallet's **−5.35 USDT** result. Candidate fees rise **2.67 USDT** and turnover **3,561 USDT**, while funding paid falls **0.72 USDT** and held time falls about 120 hours. Reduced time in the market therefore did not deliver a better wealth or daily-tail path.

The fixed-size price sensitivity remains narrow and non-executable. An additional adverse BUY or SELL price move of about **0.98%** on the 2020Q3 candidate's changed macro leg would erase its small final-wallet gain; about **0.17%** improvement in the 2023Q1 candidate's changed macro leg would be needed just to match its baseline final wallet, holding quantity and the rest of the path fixed. In 2023Q1, the larger stop quantity also makes any given per-BTC stop gap more costly. These are arithmetic price-space checks, **not** measured bid/ask, queue, latency, real stop slippage or valid thresholds. The simulator still assumes last-print IOC liquidity and a stop print exactly at its trigger.

Across the four selected original shock opportunities, the earlier 2020Q1/2021Q2 pairs prevented entry for their entire quarters and improved their local proxy results. The two newly completed pairs **reentered**; one had only a small local gain with worse sampled tail, and the other lost wealth and worsened sampled tail. No observed winning campaign was entirely omitted in these four windows, but 2023Q1's later winner became smaller. These independent quarter wallets must not be added into an annual CAGR or called forward validation. The earlier full no-macro route failed its separate drawdown/slippage criteria and is not rerun here.

**Decision:** the frozen entry deferral fails its own necessary local wealth/tail check in the complete four-opportunity review. Its one favorable later reentry is small and tail-adverse; the other exposes the unchanged risk-to-stop sizing interaction and later winner cost. Do not change the threshold, consume the opportunity permanently, increase leverage, run 795 sessions, or port this rule into `main`. The original 150% CAGR / <50% drawdown reference remains unmet. Further historical replay of this same candidate is not a justified next experiment; qualified forward manual observations or native executable-cost evidence would address different remaining questions.
